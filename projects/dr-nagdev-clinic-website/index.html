Dr. Devika Nagdev Physiotherapy

A Modern Website & Practice Management Platform for Healthcare Professionals

Your clinic deserves more than just a website.

Dr. Devika Nagdev Physiotherapy is a modern, high-performance website built for a growing physiotherapy practice. Beyond showcasing services, it includes a secure admin dashboard that allows the clinic to manage content, patient engagement, and day-to-day updates—all without requiring technical knowledge.

Originally built for my sister, Dr. Devika Nagdev (PT), after she completed her Master's and opened her clinic in Green Park, this project solved a simple problem:

She wanted to focus on treating patients—not calling me every time she needed to update her website.

Built for Performance

Unlike traditional websites that require an always-running server, this platform uses a modern serverless architecture.

Frontend

  • Next.js (Static Site Generation)

Backend

  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Cloudflare D1
  • Cloudflare R2

Automation

  • Google Apps Script
  • Google Sheets Integration

The result is:

  • Extremely fast loading
  • Low hosting costs
  • High reliability
  • Minimal maintenance
  • Global CDN delivery

A Practical Trade-Off

Since the website is fully static, content updates trigger an automated Cloudflare rebuild and deployment.

This means changes typically take 2–3 minutes to appear live instead of updating instantly.

For this project, that trade-off was intentional.

In return, the platform avoids maintaining an always-on traditional server, reducing operational complexity and hosting costs—an excellent fit for a growing clinic.
