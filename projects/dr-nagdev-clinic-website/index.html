# Dr. Devika Nagdev Physiotherapy ### A Modern Website & Practice Management Platform for Healthcare Professionals **Your clinic deserves more than just a website.** Dr. Devika Nagdev Physiotherapy is a modern, high-performance website built for a growing physiotherapy practice. Beyond showcasing services, it includes a secure admin dashboard that allows the clinic to manage content, patient engagement, and day-to-day updates—all without requiring technical knowledge. Originally built for my sister, **Dr. Devika Nagdev (PT)**, after she completed her Master's and opened her clinic in Green Park, this project solved a simple problem: **She wanted to focus on treating patients—not calling me every time she needed to update her website.** --- # Why This Platform? Many small clinics rely on developers for even the smallest website changes: * Updating clinic timings * Publishing blog posts * Adding patient testimonials * Uploading new photos * Editing service descriptions Every small change becomes another phone call. This platform puts complete control back into the hands of the clinic. No coding. No developer dependency. Just log in and manage everything yourself. --- # Key Features ## 📝 Blog Management Publish educational articles whenever you want. * Rich text editor * Image support * Draft & publish workflow * SEO-friendly content Perfect for sharing treatment advice, rehabilitation tips, and clinic updates. --- ## 💬 Patient Testimonials Build trust with real patient experiences. * Add new reviews * Edit existing testimonials * Display automatically on the homepage * Rotating testimonial carousel --- ## 📱 Social Media Integration Keep your website connected with your latest content. Easily showcase: * Instagram Reels * LinkedIn Posts * Social updates without modifying website code. --- ## 📸 Media Gallery Upload and organize: * Clinic photos * Treatment sessions * Events * Workshops * Awards * Certificates No FTP or file manager required. --- ## 🩺 Patient History Maintain simple patient records directly inside the admin dashboard. Features include: * Patient profiles * Dated consultation notes * Visit history * Treatment progress tracking Designed as a lightweight internal reference system for the clinic. --- ## 📋 Lead Management Every enquiry matters. Google Form submissions automatically appear inside the admin dashboard. No manual copying. No checking emails repeatedly. The entire enquiry pipeline stays organized in one place. --- ## 🏆 Complete Website CMS Almost every section of the website can be updated without technical assistance. Manage: * Services * Events * Awards * Homepage content * About section * Contact details * Clinic timings * Images * Testimonials All from a simple admin interface. --- # Built for Performance Unlike traditional websites that require an always-running server, this platform uses a modern serverless architecture. ### Frontend * Next.js (Static Site Generation) ### Backend * Cloudflare Workers * Cloudflare D1 * Cloudflare R2 ### Automation * Google Apps Script * Google Sheets Integration The result is: * Extremely fast loading * Low hosting costs * High reliability * Minimal maintenance * Global CDN delivery --- # A Practical Trade-Off Since the website is fully static, content updates trigger an automated Cloudflare rebuild and deployment. This means changes typically take **2–3 minutes** to appear live instead of updating instantly. For this project, that trade-off was intentional. In return, the platform avoids maintaining an always-on traditional server, reducing operational complexity and hosting costs—an excellent fit for a growing clinic. --- # What I Learned This project became much more than building a clinic website. It gave me hands-on experience with: * Cloudflare Workers * Cloudflare D1 * Cloudflare R2 * Serverless architecture * Google Apps Script * Google Sheets automation * Static deployment workflows * Secure admin authentication * Production-grade CMS design Most importantly, it demonstrated that modern serverless technologies are mature enough to power real-world business applications—not just personal projects. --- # Ideal For This platform can easily be adapted for: * Physiotherapy Clinics * Doctors * Dentists * Nutritionists * Psychologists * Consultants * Coaches * Lawyers * CA Firms * Small Businesses * Startups * Solo Professionals Any business that wants a fast website with an easy-to-manage admin panel. --- # Looking for Something Similar?
