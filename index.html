About

I’m a passionate, high-agency engineer with a diverse background in automation, AI/ML deployment and quality assurance — thriving at the intersection of innovation and efficiency. My journey began with technical drawings and hands-on projects during graduation, followed by product development and research automation at IIT Delhi. I contributed to the R&D of high-end oscilloscopes at Keysight Technologies, sharpening my skills in regression testing and automation.

With expertise in Python, Selenium, Docker, Kubernetes and Linux — skills I applied to deploy and automate warehouse web applications at GreyOrange — I later expanded into the VLSI domain through a PG Diploma from IISc Bangalore, gaining hands-on chip-design experience. Today I channel all of that energy into creating production-ready deployments and performing rigorous QA on them at the Centre of Artificial Intelligence, IIIT Delhi. As a patent holder, I bring a creative edge to problem-solving and system optimization. Let’s connect to build something impactful.

IoT Smart Liquid Food Dispenser

Completed Jan 2017

It was my Final Year Project .An IOT liquid food dispenser system for animals has been developed to provide liquid food and indirectly solid food as well with monitoring system. However, typical existing automatic food dispenser systems provide only solid food without monitoring capabilities.This device consist of novel automated liquid food system that combines feed dispensing and monitoring food level as one process. The system designed is eco friendly as it disposes the waste water which can be used in watering a plant that can be used as a another kind of food for animals .Not only this the system has also featured an IOT technology for monitoring purpose in the system which provide real time sensor value on webpage using an API. This project if implemented for welfare of stray animals (dogs, cat, birds etc) or preventing biodiversity loss then it can help us to achieve smart city in an environment sector. This IOT Liquid food dispenser device can be used for commercial (by users having pet) as well as for social welfare (for stray animals, birds).
